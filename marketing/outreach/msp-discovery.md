# MSP discovery outreach (wl1)

The goal is to find out which region's MSPs will pilot Coriqo. Every contact
is a conversation to learn from, not a pitch. The design-partner offer comes
up on the call, never in writing.

## What "human-first" means here

1. **Look before you write.** Step 1 is a LinkedIn profile visit. Read their
   posts and note one real detail. If you can't find one, skip the person.
2. **Connect with no pitch.** The connection note asks for their view and
   says we're not selling yet.
3. **Every email is sent by hand.** All email steps are *manual*: Apollo
   drafts them and a person adds a personal first line, reads the email and
   clicks send. Nothing goes out automatically.
4. **Ask, don't sell.** The only ask is 15 minutes of their opinion. "Nobody
   cares" is a valid answer and we say so.
5. **One person per company.** Search results often return 2–3 people at the
   same MSP. Pick the most senior one and drop the rest.
6. **Low volume.** Cap of 15 emails a day per sequence (10 for
   distributors). Start at 10 contacts per region, not hundreds.
7. **No links, no attachments, no tracking pixels** in the first email.
   Plain text from a real person.

### De-AI pass

The earlier draft in chat was edited against the "signs of AI writing"
checklist before going into Apollo:

| Original | Issue | Now |
|---|---|---|
| "We're building Coriqo for exactly that: a managed browser extension that records…" | Colon-then-reveal, product pitch in the first email | Removed. The first email asks one question and describes no features. |
| "Worth 20 minutes to see if it fits?" | Stock sales phrase | "Could I get 15 minutes of your view on it? A 'nobody cares' answer is useful too." |
| "…with GDPR and EU AI Act evidence built in" | Feature claim for something not built yet | Replaced by a question about what they're seeing |
| "We're picking 3 MSPs as design partners: first client free…" | Offer in a cold email reads as a sales sequence | Moved to the call |
| Em-dash asides, "not X, but Y" framing | Common AI tells | None in any template |

Emails are 45–80 words. Subject lines are lower-key and under five words.

- [ ] **Enrich only who you'll contact.** Adding people costs Apollo credits
      (2,600 available). Start with 10 per region = 50 credits.

## On the call (15 minutes)

Listen for 12 minutes and talk for 3. Ask:

1. Has any client or insurer asked you about staff AI use? What exactly did
   they ask?
2. What do you do about it today? Any tool, policy template, or nothing?
3. If you could hand each client a monthly "here's what your staff put into
   AI" report, would they pay for it? Would you resell it?
4. What would stop you rolling out a new agent or browser extension to
   client machines?
5. What per-seat price would feel like a no-brainer, and what would feel
   like too much?

Only if they're clearly interested: "We're picking three MSPs as design
partners. Your first client is free while we build it with you, then per-seat
pricing you mark up. Would you want to be one?"
